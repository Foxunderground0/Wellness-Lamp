Component gallery

Every component of the app, in every state the prototype draws. Each picture is a phone from FINAL_screens.html (the same app code, opened in the same state), cut down to one component with a little of what surrounds it, shown at 1x (the files are 2x). The tag under each picture is the screen it comes from: it opens that phone, where anything that moves can be seen moving. Whole screens show the full phone at half size. The specs are in DESIGN_SYSTEM.md (the link under each name goes to its entry); the values in tokens.json.

A few states need a tap first; their caption says what was tapped. A dashed box is a state the prototype doesn’t draw.
